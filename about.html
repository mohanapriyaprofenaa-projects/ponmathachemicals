Company overview

A Reliable Partner for Your Chemical Requirements

At Ponmatha Chemicals, we bring together a diverse range of chemical products and supply solutions to support businesses in the textile printing and allied industries. With a strong focus on chemical trading and manufacturing capabilities, we help customers access the products they need through a dependable business partnership.

From water-based printing products and specialty inks to pigments, bases, and printing accessories, our portfolio is designed to serve a wide range of industrial requirements.

Where we are headed

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide customers with access to a diverse range of chemical products through dependable trading and manufacturing operations, responsive service, and a customer-oriented approach.

We strive to build lasting business relationships by understanding industrial requirements and helping customers identify suitable products for their applications.

Our Vision

To grow into a trusted and recognized chemical supply partner in Tiruppur and beyond, known for our broad product range, responsible business practices, and commitment to long-term customer relationships.

We aspire to strengthen our presence in the chemical industry by expanding our portfolio, developing our capabilities, and creating value for the businesses we serve.
